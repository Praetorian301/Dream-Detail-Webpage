FAQ

Before you book

Can't see your question? Add it to the notes on your request, or message us on Instagram.

Do you come to my suburb?

Detailing packages are mobile across Perth metro. Use the postcode checker. If you're further out, send a request anyway and we'll let you know.

Why is paint correction done at the workshop?

Correction needs controlled lighting to reveal every defect, plus shelter from sun and dust. Our Floreat workshop is set up for exactly that. Drop the car off, or we'll pick it up and return it at no extra cost.

Which paint correction level do I need?

Two stage suits most cars and gives the best result for the time spent. Single stage lifts gloss but won't remove deeper scratches. Three stage is for dark paint or a near-showroom finish; the difference over two stage is subtle in normal light. We'll recommend one after a quick look.

How long does it take?

A Complete Detail is done the same day. Most paint correction takes about two days; bigger jobs involving coatings or restoration can take three days or more, because we don't rush them.

How do I get an accurate price?

Send the make, model and year with a few photos and we'll give you a rough estimate. For paint correction we confirm the exact price after a 10-minute inspection, and you don't need to be home for it.

What do you need from me for a mobile detail?

Just access to a power outlet and a water tap, as we bring our own hose and extension leads. Please clear out any personal items, and if you're out, leave the keys somewhere safe.
