Where we work

All over Perth, plus a Floreat workshop

Detailing packages are fully mobile across Perth: home, work or wherever the car is parked. Paint correction, ceramic coatings and restoration are done at our Floreat workshop, with free pick-up and return.

Booking hours
  • Mobile jobs: all we need is a power outlet and a tap. We bring our own hose and extension leads.
  • Not home? Leave the keys somewhere safe and we'll get started.
  • Workshop jobs: drop off in Floreat, or we pick up and return the car at no extra cost.
How booking works

Four steps, no phone tag

  1. Request

    Pick a service and tell us the make, model and year. A few photos help us quote accurately.

  2. Quote

    We'll send a rough price based on your photos. For paint correction, a quick 10-minute inspection confirms it, and you don't need to be home.

  3. Detail

    We arrive with everything except power and water. Correction jobs are done in Floreat: drop the car off, or we'll collect it for free.

  4. Handover

    Look over the finished car with us, or come back to find it ready. If you're out, just leave the keys somewhere safe.
